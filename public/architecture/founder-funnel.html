The founder-led difference

In a founder-led business the founder is the funnel — trust transfers from one person (the founder's story, voice and stage presence), so the top of funnel is mostly their own content, not faceless ads.

And the bottom isn't a single purchase — it's a value ladder: a buyer ascends from free content to a low-ticket course to deep programs, a live event, and finally high-ticket coaching. Most revenue lives at the top of the ladder; the front end mainly exists to find and warm buyers.

The founder's value ladder

Four price tiers, free to $100k. Green badges show the illustrative share of each tier that climbs to the next.

Bottom = free entry, top = the $100k mastermind. Green numbers are the step rate — the share of the tier below that ascends to the next. The grey column on the right is the cumulative share of the whole audience that reaches each rung; it compounds fast, so coaching sits near 0.0012% — roughly 1 in 83,000. That is why the high tiers, though tiny, drive most of the revenue.

Why the webinar is the big one

A webinar lead has done two things — handed over an email and committed 60–90 minutes of attention. That makes it far higher-intent than a cold ebook download, so it converts to a sale at a much higher rate. For the founder, the Mind Hack webinar → Mind Hack Program is the core acquisition engine: it captures the lead, teaches the framework, builds trust in their voice, and pitches the entry offer all in one sitting. Scale registrations and you scale revenue.

Acronyms, definitions & formulas

Cost and performance metrics include the calculation. The HS badge marks terms that are HubSpot-popularized, not universal.

Ecosystem jargon — what's universal vs tool-specific

Most terms here are universal across every platform: MQL, SQL, SAL, PQL, ICP, CPM, CPC, CPL, CPA, CAC, CTR, CVR, ROAS, ROI, LTV, NRR, NPS, AIDA, CTA, KPI.

HS HubSpot / B2B-CRM jargon: TOFU / MOFU / BOFU (HubSpot popularized these), plus lifecycle stage and the labels Subscriber and Evangelist, and Marketing Contacts (a billing tier).

Funnel-builder / info-product jargon — the founder's actual world (ClickFunnels-style): value ladder, SLO, VSL, EPC, show rate, ascension. Powerful here, but don't assume a corporate B2B team uses them.

Cost metrics
CPM Cost per mille
Cost per 1,000 ad impressions.
(cost ÷ impressions) × 1,000
CPC Cost per click
Average cost for each click.
total cost ÷ total clicks
CPL Cost per lead
Spend to add one email subscriber or webinar registrant.
total spend ÷ number of leads
CPA Cost per acquisition
Cost per defined conversion (e.g. a Mind Hack Program buyer).
total cost ÷ number of conversions
CAC Customer acquisition cost
Fully-loaded cost to win one paying customer.
(sales + marketing spend) ÷ new customers
Performance & value
CTR Click-through rate
Share of viewers who clicked.
(clicks ÷ impressions) × 100
CVR Conversion rate
Share who took the desired action.
(conversions ÷ visitors or leads) × 100
ROAS Return on ad spend
Revenue per dollar of ads (a multiple, e.g. 4×).
revenue from ads ÷ ad spend
ROI Return on investment
Profit relative to cost.
((revenue − cost) ÷ cost) × 100
LTV / CLV (Customer) lifetime value
Total value a customer brings as they ascend the ladder (× margin for profit-based LTV).
avg order value × orders per year × lifespan (yrs)
LTV:CAC Lifetime value to CAC ratio
Unit-economics health check; 3:1 is a common benchmark.
LTV ÷ CAC
NPS Net promoter score
Loyalty score from −100 to +100 (9–10 promoters, 0–6 detractors).
% promoters − % detractors
NRR Net revenue retention
Revenue kept plus expansion from existing customers; MRR = monthly recurring revenue.
(start MRR + expansion − contraction − churn) ÷ start MRR × 100

Read it top to bottom: the founder's own content casts the widest net, the Mind Hack ebook and webinar convert attention into email leads, and the value ladder turns a small front-end buyer into a high-ticket client over time. The webinar is the engine — it captures the lead and makes the sale in one sitting. The number that ties it together is LTV:CAC: as long as a buyer's lifetime value across the ladder beats the cost to acquire them, every new lead is worth getting.
