Lead Hygiene, Migration, and Compliance System

A client-ready artifact that combines the architecture, operating model, migration roadmap, compliance layer, customer journey, cost model, and hiring plan into one document. Recommended stack: n8n Cloud for orchestration, CrewAI/FastAPI for hygiene logic, Supabase for audit + quarantine + vector storage, and HubSpot as target CRM.

Executive Summary

Recommendation

Put a hygiene layer in front of HubSpot so every source passes through validation, enrichment, scoring, and quarantine before CRM write. Use n8n Cloud as the harness, CrewAI/FastAPI for the judgment layer, and Supabase for auditability.

Why

Dirty data should be stopped before it enters the CRM. This improves routing, sales efficiency, compliance, reporting, and migration quality. Founder-led businesses benefit more from quality than raw speed.

Expected Cost

Estimated monthly operating cost: $495–$1,555, excluding HubSpot seats. This is typically cheaper than heavy per-lead pricing while offering more control and cleaner audit trails.

How the Stack Works Together

OpenClaw = overall product/app shell → n8n = automation harness and orchestration → CrewAI/FastAPI = hygiene and compliance judgment layer → Supabase = storage, logs, quarantine, vectors → HubSpot = clean CRM destination

Think of n8n as the traffic controller, CrewAI as the analyst, Supabase as the evidence locker, and HubSpot as the destination system. OpenClaw can remain the shell around this if that is how the client wants to package the experience.

Lead Journey Narrative

A lead submits a form at night. The payload hits the n8n webhook, gets normalized into a canonical schema, is deduplicated, and gets basic enrichment. The record is then sent to CrewAI, where one layer maps the fields, another validates phone, email, consent, and suppression, another enriches the record with business and context data, and a scoring layer decides whether it is ready.

If the record passes the quality threshold, it is written into HubSpot with clean fields and the right workflow starts immediately. If it fails, it is not allowed into the CRM. It goes to quarantine, a human reviews it, and only approved records are released forward. From the lead’s perspective, the experience feels fast, relevant, and personalized.

Compliance and Governance

Governance Model

  • Capture proof of consent and compliance checks in an append-only audit log.
  • Enforce non-bypassable rules in n8n before any CRM write or outbound action.
  • Monitor regulatory drift through TCPA / email-compliance feeds and alert on material changes.

Must-Have Compliance Inputs

  • TrustedForm or equivalent proof-of-consent capture for TCPA defensibility.
  • National + state DNC and known-litigator suppression scrubs before any outbound action.
  • Regulatory-change feeds for TCPA / FCC / FTC (Telemarketing Sales Rule) and CAN-SPAM email rules.
