What This Is Really Telling You

The read on this campaign

On the surface the numbers look healthy — over a thousand dials in two days at almost no cost. But volume is masking the real story. This was a dialing exercise, not a selling exercise. More than eight in ten calls never reached a live, interested human: they landed in voicemail, hit a dead number, or bounced off a busy signal. The low cost isn't a win to celebrate; it's a symptom of how few real conversations actually happened.

  • Reach, not conversion, is the bottleneck. A 53.9% "connection" rate is really a voicemail-drop rate — average talk time of ~5.8s means almost no one is being spoken to. The list and the dialing strategy, not the pitch, are where value is leaking.
  • List quality is quietly draining the campaign. 18.3% bad numbers means roughly 1 in every 5 dials was dead on arrival. Scrubbing the list is the single highest-leverage fix before spending another hour on the phones.
  • Effort is going to overhead, not selling. After-call work (2.92 hrs) is outrunning actual talk time (1.69 hrs). Reps are spending more time logging calls than talking to prospects — a process problem worth automating away.

Key Insights & Recommendations

Strengths

  • Cost Efficiency: Extremely low cost per call ($0.0012) indicates an efficient dialing system
  • High Activity: 1,047 calls in 2 days shows strong campaign momentum
  • Top Performers: Rep A and Rep B driving majority of volume (50%)
  • Peak Performance: 2:00 PM shows highest activity (349 calls)

Areas for Improvement

  • Data Quality: 18.3% bad numbers (192 calls) — clean list needed to improve efficiency
  • Low Talk Time: Average 5.8s suggests quick voicemail drops — consider longer messages
  • Connection Rate: 53.9% productive calls — could improve with callback strategies
  • Low Conversion: Only 1 appointment set from 1,047 calls (0.1%)
  • After Call Work: 2.92 hrs ACW vs 1.69 hrs talk time — streamline processes

Action Items

  • List Hygiene: Scrub contact list to remove bad numbers before next campaign
  • Script Review: Evaluate voicemail script — extremely low conversion suggests poor messaging
  • Training: Focus on agents with lower volume (Rep E, Rep F) to match top performers
  • Timing: Concentrate efforts during the 1–3 PM window for maximum reach
  • Process Efficiency: Reduce ACW time through automation or simplified logging
  • Follow-up Strategy: Implement callback strategy for voicemails and no-answers
