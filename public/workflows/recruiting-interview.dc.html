The two delivery packages

Included

Capture & Deliver

Everything lands in a repository; your team processes it however you want.

1Bundle the candidate package — video answers + résumé/CV + form details.
2Deposit to a per-role Google Drive folder, logged in a shared tracking sheet.
3Notify the hiring team with a link.
4Hand off — you review and process on your own terms.
Optional add-on

AI Scoring & ATS

Everything in Base, plus automated scoring and hand-off into your ATS.

1Transcribe each recorded answer.
2AI score & rank answers against the role’s rubric.
3Summarize — strengths, flags, and highlight moments.
4Sync to your ATS/HRIS with scores + links on the record.
5Ranked shortlist + reviewer dashboard + auto-notifications.

Both packages start from the same captured recording — the add-on layers scoring on top, with no change to the candidate experience.
